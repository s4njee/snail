# Bundled fonts

Self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. No runtime fetch (plan.md §1.3).

| File | Family | Weights | Upstream |
|---|---|---|---|
| `InstrumentSans-{400,500,600,700}.ttf` | Instrument Sans | 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 | google/fonts `ofl/instrumentsans` (variable instanced with fonttools) |
| `Newsreader-{400,500}.ttf` | Newsreader | 400 / 500 | google/fonts `ofl/newsreader` (variable instanced, `opsz=16`) |
| `DMMono-{Regular,Medium}.ttf` | DM Mono | 400 / 500 | google/fonts `ofl/dmmono` |

All three are OFL-1.1. The OFL text for each family is in the upstream repository; redistribute the
app with those notices.
